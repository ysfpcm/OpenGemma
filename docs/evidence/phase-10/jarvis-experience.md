# Phase 10 JARVIS experience assessment

## Presence

Ophanim presents the correction, its durable relationship/procedural evidence,
the generated procedure, the replay result, the shadow boundary, and the
promotion decision as one causal learning record. The Learning Laboratory is a
bounded subsystem rather than a second authority.

## Continuity

Corrections, adaptations, candidates, replay runs, regression results, shadow
runs, decisions, signed artifacts, runtime pointers, and rollback records
survive restart in the Phase 10 ledger. Duplicate correction delivery is
idempotent. An interrupted replay is recovered as failed/unknown rather than
silently treated as success.

## Initiative

Ophanim extracts a procedure and candidate from Marc's correction immediately,
then proactively runs the required deterministic checks. It does not silently
activate the result: sandboxing, replay, shadow, Marc approval, staging, and
activation remain distinct visible boundaries.

## Competence

The tangible fixture improves only the sunset and overnight departure behavior,
while preserving daytime, guest-present, general Away, and explicit contradictory
command behavior. The Codex candidate is tested across historical mission
fixtures and rejected when it forgets the approval boundary. These are
deterministic capability and regression demonstrations, not claims of live
success.

## Trust

Sensitive model experiments require explicit privacy-reviewed consent. Replay
and shadow records preserve provenance, source version, disagreement, stale
source, budget, cancellation, protected-capability, and forgetting state.
Promotion requires Marc where configured, signed reversible artifacts, and
verified rollback. No candidate creates Guardian authority, touches the primary
workspace, or causes a live external effect.

## Regressions and unchanged dimensions

No Phase 10 regression was found in its focused acceptance lane or the complete
acceptance suite. The neighboring `tests/learning` sweep contains unrelated
pre-existing checkpoint/path/device/Windows-path failures; none exercise the
Phase 10 laboratory package. Phase 11 artifacts exist separately in the dirty
worktree, but no Phase 11 file was changed during this review and its acceptance
remains pending.
