# Phase 9 JARVIS experience assessment

## Presence

Ophanim presents one decision receipt: it names the selected departure
strategy, the rejected candidates, and the predicted observations that drove
the choice. Specialists, simulations, and the digital twin remain subordinate
evidence rather than separate authorities.

## Continuity

Candidates, predictions, simulations, observations, calibrations, and the
decision are durable in SQLite. Duplicate saves are idempotent. Restart
recovery preserves an interrupted simulation as unknown rather than inventing
a real-world outcome.

## Initiative

The engine generates three materially different candidates and proactively
rejects the full-away candidate before Guardian. It modifies the presence-aware
candidate after the cover contradiction and selects a consent-preserving
degraded-service option. No candidate executes by itself.

## Competence

The deterministic run handles all four seeded complications: it treats cover
feedback as contradictory, accounts for another person at home, refuses to
infer physical attendance from an address on a likely remote event, and uses a
labeled traffic fallback during outage. The score includes seven required
dimensions and the prediction ledger supports later replay calibration.

## Trust

The Guardian boundary revalidates only valid candidates and emits no grants or
effects. Digital-twin state is separate from live state. Simulated failure is
marked simulation-scoped, and the isolated Codex candidate's seeded failure
leaves the primary workspace unchanged. No live or consequential external
effect occurred.

## Regressions and deliberately unchanged scope

The Phase 0–8 acceptance behavior was not intentionally changed. The focused
Phase 9 review suite passed 9 tests, the complete acceptance suite passed 134
tests, and the targeted Phase 3/7 regression passed 49 tests. The repository's
pre-existing uncommitted changes and temporary evidence directories were
preserved. Phase 10 learning/promotion behavior was deliberately not begun.

## Acceptance record

Marc explicitly accepted Phase 9 on 2026-08-16 (America/New_York) for the
deterministic local slice. Live adapters, ledger views, held-out replay
measurement, and manual review of future live policies remain deferred.
